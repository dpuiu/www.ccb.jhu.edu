# Web Servers and Databases 


(chess-app)=
## [CHESS App](https://web.idies.jhu.edu/chess_app)

A unified resource for exploring the CHESS human genome annotation.

**Categories:** genome-annotation, human-genome  
**Publication:** [PMC10614308](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10614308/)



(isoform-io)=
## [Isoform.io](https://www.isoform.io/)

3D-Beacons decreases the gap between protein sequences and structures through a federated network of protein structure data resources.

**Categories:** protein-structure, structural-biology  
**Publication:** [PMC9709962](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9709962/)



(hiv-atlas)=
## [HIV Atlas](https://ccb.jhu.edu/HIV_Atlas/)

HIV Atlas provides comprehensive transcriptome annotation of thousands of HIV-1 genomes.

**Categories:** viral-genomics, transcriptomics, hiv  
**Publication:** [PMC12485963](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12485963/)



(chess)=
## [CHESS](https://github.com/dpuiu/chess/wiki/)

A new catalog of human genes based on nearly 10,000 RNA sequencing experiments.

**Categories:** genome-annotation, transcriptomics, rna-seq  
**Publication:** [PMC6260756](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6260756/)



(t2t-chm13)=
## [T2T-CHM13 Annotation](https://github.com/salzberg-lab/T2T)

RefSeq annotation of the CHM13 genome created using the Liftoff program.

**Categories:** genome-annotation, human-genome, reference-genome  
**Publication:** [PMC8289374](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8289374/)

Antibiotic Resistance Genes Database.

**Categories:** antibiotic-resistance, bacterial-genomics
