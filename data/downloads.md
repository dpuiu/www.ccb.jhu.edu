## GAGE

[GAGE](https://gage.cbcb.umd.edu/) and [GAGE-B](https://ccb.jhu.edu/gage_b/) are
evaluations of contiguity and accuracy of assemblies that are generated by some
of most commonly used genome assemblers. GAGE-B (for assemblies of bacterial
organisms) follows the standards set by GAGE.

## Drosophila (fruit fly) endosymbionts

Steven Salzberg and colleagues identified the genome sequence of the bacterial
endosymbiont *Wolbachia* within publicly available sequencing data from several
species of fruit flies. The discovery was reported in the open-access journal
*Genome Biology*:

> Salzberg, S.L., Hotopp, J.C., Delcher, A.L., Pop, M., Smith, D.R.,
> Eisen, M.B., Nelson, W.C. (2005).  
> *Serendipitous discovery of Wolbachia genomes in multiple Drosophila species.*  
> **Genome Biology** 6(3):R23.  
> [PubMed](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/query.fcgi?cmd=Retrieve&db=PubMed&dopt=Citation&list_uids=15774024)

The endosymbiont genome assemblies were generated using
[AMOScmp](https://amos.sourceforge.net/wiki/index.php?title=AMOScmp).
