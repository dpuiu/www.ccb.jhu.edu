# EuPathDB 


(eupathdb54)=
## [EuPathDB54](https://github.com/dpuiu/eupathDB/)

Latest curated database of eukaryotic pathogen genomes. Contains ~504 genomes from major eukaryotic pathogen groups and has been processed to remove contaminating and low-complexity sequences. A pre-built Kraken 2 database and the individual genome collections are provided.


**Categories:** eukaryotic-pathogens, genome-database, metagenomics, kraken2-database  
**Publication:** [PMC6034898](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6034898/)

(eupathdb28)=
## [EuPathDB28](https://github.com/dpuiu/eupathDB/)

Earlier curated collection of eukaryotic pathogen genomes. Contains 245 genomes processed to remove contaminating and low-complexity sequences. The collection was distributed as a genome library and a pre-built Kraken 2 database.


**Categories:** metagenomics, eukaryotic-pathogens, kraken2-database, genome-database  
**Publication:** [PMC6034898](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6034898/)
