### Drosophila (fruit fly) endosymbionts

Steven Salzberg and colleagues identified the sequence of the bacterial
endosymbiont [Wolbachia]{style="font-style: italic;"} within the
publicly available sequence data of several species of fruit fly. These
results were reported in the open access journal *Genome Biology*:\
